01 / About me

I am strongest where acquisition, measurement and operations meet.

My work is not limited to launching ads. I structure campaigns, build tracking, diagnose weak funnels, connect lead sources to CRM systems, automate repetitive marketing operations, improve organic visibility and turn reporting into decisions.

Performance first.

I focus on the numbers that matter: conversions, cost per acquisition, lead quality, revenue signals, search demand and the path from spend to business outcome.

Then fix the system behind it.

If tracking is broken, leads are lost, CRM data is messy or follow-up is slow, media efficiency has a ceiling. I work on those problems too.
